## (a) Verdict

History does not show an implanted backdoor, a forged maintainer, or a bot editing anything beyond dependency pins and the CA bundle. The people who can change release integrity are still Jay Lee and Ross Scroggs, and they have been for years.

The concrete risk is pinning. Official builds trust a moving tag for Python and OpenSSL (`jay0lee/python-openssl-latest@v1`), an unpinned wheel repo (`jay0lee/cryptography-wheels`), and `https://sh.rustup.rs` inside the macOS signing job. The CA bundle updates itself on `main` under a generic unsigned identity. Those are maintainer-operated paths with weak locks, not a new contributor's drop.
